Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between Pythagorean and Chaldean numerology?

Pythagorean numerology uses a Western 1-9 system where letters A-Z are mapped sequentially. Chaldean numerology uses a phonetically-based 1-8 system that does not assign the number 9 to letters unless they form a final name calculation.

Is numerology scientifically proven?

No, numerology is a symbolic framework and reflective tool used for personal contemplation and analysis. It is not an empirical science and should not be used to make financial, legal, medical, or relationship decisions.
